During the talk, Dr. Zahari Othman shared his knowledge about high-order thinking skills (HOTS) and how its roles are crucial in developing a greater mindset and cognitive adaptability. With his extensive experience as the Director of Center for Teaching Thinking & Innovation (CenT – TI), Dr. Zahari also highlighted the problem with modern graduates who are struggling to apply the foundation of critical thinking to solve complex, real-world problems. To address this concern, he introduced different concepts of critical thinking which could be applied for the use of industry. He later emphasized on the concept of metacognition which is equivalent to the brain’s ability to step back and reflect on the way of thinking. This concept also helps to turn passive learners into strategic problem-solvers.
