There is a massive difference between the basic thinking we use to get through the day, like checking if a cashier gave us the right change and the higher-order thinking we need to solve complex problems, for example math questions (Othman, 2026). This kind of advanced mental work usually falls into four specific categories like critical, creative, mathematical and scientific thinking (Othman, 2026). To actually be good at this, we have to mix higher-order thinking with productive mental habits and metacognition, which is being actively aware of how we process our own thoughts. It has to be taught and trained, because we aren’t naturally born with these advanced problem solving skills (Othman, 2026).
